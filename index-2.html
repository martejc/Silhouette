Dx diagnosis

Scrubs haven't moved in 30 years.

Report

Built for healthcare.
Designed to change the game.

SILHOUETTE started on a twelve hour shift when the founder decided he was tired of scrubs fitting like a paper bag, losing their shape after a couple washes, and having no identity to them. So he created a whole new design - drawn, created, and tailored with the same eye he had for fashion outside of the hospital.

"We didn't want just any other scrub. We wanted something that would change the scene."

— Design Team
